# Sendforsign Landing Page

The official landing page for Sendforsign - the eSignature platform for your Business AI Agent.

## About Sendforsign

Sendforsign is the platform to roll out AI contracts to all of your teams and clients. We enable developers to build, embed and deploy the most custom eSign workflows.

### Key Features

- **eSignature Features**: Document & signing, contract templates, multi-signer support, in-person & remote PDF signing
- **Workflow & Automation**: Contract builder, approvals, webhooks, and integrations with n8n and Make
- **Security & Compliance**: Complete audit trail, timestamping, and signature certificates
- **API+AI First**: Simplify API requests with our unified MCP and receive unified AI responses
- **React Components**: Pre-built components for contract management, templates, and AI assistant
- **Real-time Updates**: Webhooks for instant notifications on contract events

## What We Offer

Sendforsign helps teams:
- Close contracts faster
- Reduce errors
- Maintain full legal evidence
- Integrate effortlessly with existing workflows

Transform how you create and manage contracts with AI-powered contract generation, flexible AI API, and embeddable components that provide a native experience.
